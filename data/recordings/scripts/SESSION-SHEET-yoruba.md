# Ntina recording session — Yoruba (yo-en)

One page, front to back, for whoever is holding the phone. Print it or open it on a second phone during the session.

## Four rules — read this before you start

1. **Never correct the speaker.** Errors and hesitation are the data.
2. **If someone cannot read an item, record the attempt or the silence anyway. Do not skip the row.**
3. **One file per utterance.** Never one long recording per session.
4. **No real names** in any filename or in `metadata.csv` — use the `speaker_id` only.

**Children will stall or fail on paragraph and story items — this is expected at this reading level, not a mistake. Record it anyway (see rule 2).**

## 3. Set B — free spoken answers (10 questions, READ ALOUD to the speaker)

Read each question aloud to the speaker. The speaker reads nothing and answers freely, in their own words. Record their answer, not the question.

1. Sọ fún mi nípa ilé-ìwé rẹ.
2. Kí ni o ṣe lánàá lẹ́yìn ilé-ìwé?
3. Báwo ni o ṣe ń lọ sí ọjà láti ilé?
4. Ẹ̀kọ́ wo ni o fẹ́ràn jùlọ, kí ló sì dé?
5. Sọ fún mi nípa àkókò kan tí o ràn ẹnìkan lọ́wọ́.
6. Ka ní ohùn sókè láti ọ̀kan dé ogún.
7. Mélòó ni mẹ́ẹ̀ẹ́dógún kún èje? Sọ fún mi bí o ṣe rí ìdáhùn náà.
8. Sọ fún mi ohun kan tó ṣẹlẹ̀ ní ilé-ìwé ní ọ̀sẹ̀ yìí.
9. Kí ni o fẹ́ ṣe nígbà tí o bá dàgbà?
10. Ṣàlàyé eré kan tí o ń ṣeré pẹ̀lú àwọn ọ̀rẹ́ rẹ, àti òfin rẹ̀.
